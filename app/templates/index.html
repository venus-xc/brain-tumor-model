System Architecture & Algorithmic Foundations

Implementation details corresponding directly to the research requirements articulated in the seminar abstract and IEEE literature.

1. Squeeze-and-Excitation ResNet Classifier

Channel attention recalibration using Global Average Pooling and reduction ratio r=16 (Hu et al., IEEE TPAMI). Accurately distinguishes Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary Adenoma, and Healthy Brain Parenchyma with Softmax calibration.

2. Attention U-Net with Skip Gates

Additive spatial Attention Gates (Oktay et al., IEEE TMI) embedded on skip connections. Dynamically filters out irrelevant background tissue (skull bone, ventricles) while emphasizing true tumor margins.

3. Dual Explainable AI (Grad-CAM & Grad-CAM++)

Computes first-order and higher-order partial derivative weights (Chattopadhyay et al., IEEE TIP). Provides high-resolution saliency maps resolving multi-focal lesions and tiny sellar microadenomas.

4. Longitudinal RANO Modeling

Multi-timepoint tracking across patient therapy timelines. Computes volumetric delta (ΔV), surface area shifts, doubling rates, and categorizes clinical response into Complete Response, Partial Response, Stable Disease, or Progression.
