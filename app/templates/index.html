System Architecture & Methodology

Technical formulation of classification, segmentation, interpretability, and quantitative follow-up analysis.

1. Classification Backbones

Comparison across 4 distinct paradigms: classical HOG + SVM (1,764-dim HOG with PCA-150 and RBF kernel), baseline Custom CNN (4 stages, [32, 64, 128, 256]), SE-CNN with Squeeze-and-Excitation channel recalibration (r=16), and fine-tuned EfficientNet-B0 (transfer learning from ImageNet).

2. Biomedical Lesion Segmentation

Biomedical U-Net and Attention U-Net (Oktay et al., 2018) trained on 256×256 single-channel T1-CE slices with BCEWithLogits + Soft Dice loss. Additive Attention Gates selectively scale feature activations along skip connections to suppress non-tumor parenchyma.

3. Dual Explainable AI (XAI)

First-order Grad-CAM and higher-order Grad-CAM++ computed via autograd gradients at the final convolutional feature extractor. Verifies that classification decisions originate from the true lesion rather than background skull artifacts.

4. 2D Planar Morphometrics & RANO

Strict 2D measurements on index slices: area = N_pixels × Δx × Δy, longest diameter d1 (PCA major axis), perpendicular diameter d2, and perpendicular product P = d1 × d2. Temporal response categorised under RANO: PR ≤ -50%, PD ≥ +25%, CR (complete resolution), SD (stable).
