System Architecture & Algorithmic Foundations

Implementation details corresponding directly to the research requirements articulated in the seminar abstract.

1. Multi-Class CNN Classifier

Residual deep convolutional network with batch normalization and adaptive global pooling. Discerning between Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary Adenoma, and Healthy parenchyma with Softmax confidence calibration.

2. Biomedical U-Net Segmentation

Encoder-decoder symmetric architecture with skip connections preserving spatial gradient information. Accurately segments diffuse, irregular tumor margins and necrotic cores.

3. Explainable AI (Grad-CAM)

Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping hooked to the final convolutional feature maps. Projects neural attribution saliency heatmaps to eliminate black-box uncertainty for clinicians.

4. Longitudinal RANO Modeling

Tracks successive scans across patient therapy timelines. Computes volumetric delta (ΔV), surface area shifts, doubling rates, and categorizes clinical response into Complete Response, Partial Response, Stable Disease, or Progression.
