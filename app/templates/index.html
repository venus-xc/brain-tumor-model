System Architecture & Methodology

Implementation details corresponding directly to the seminar project objectives and IEEE literature benchmarks.

1. Squeeze-and-Excitation ResNet Classifier

Channel attention recalibration using Global Average Pooling and reduction ratio r=16 (Hu et al., IEEE TPAMI). Differentiates Glioma, Meningioma, Pituitary Adenoma, and Healthy Parenchyma.

2. Attention U-Net Segmentation

Additive spatial Attention Gates (Oktay et al., IEEE TMI) on skip connections. Dynamically filters out non-lesion background tissue while emphasizing true tumor boundaries.

3. Dual Explainable AI (Grad-CAM & Grad-CAM++)

Computes first-order and higher-order partial derivatives (Chattopadhyay et al., IEEE TIP). Delivers saliency heatmaps resolving multi-focal lesions and tiny sellar borders.

4. Longitudinal RANO Modeling

Multi-timepoint tracking across patient timelines. Computes volumetric delta, growth velocity, and classifies clinical response (Complete Response, Partial Response, Stable Disease, Progressive Disease).
